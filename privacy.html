Privacy Policy – Unlockhash.com

Effective Date: May 16, 2025

At Unlockhash.com, your privacy is shaped by the power and limitations of blockchain. We are committed to transparency, and because all platform activity occurs via public smart contracts, we collect and store no personal information off-chain.

1. What Data Is Stored

All data related to hash submissions, bounty values, and solver actions is stored on a public blockchain. This data is:

  • Transparent and visible to all
  • Immutable—cannot be edited or deleted
  • Accessible without involvement of Unlockhash.com

2. What Data Is Not Stored

We do not collect or store:

  • IP addresses
  • Emails or names (unless you email us directly)
  • Cookies or tracking information

3. Optional Communication

If you reach out via email, your message is kept only to respond to your inquiry and is never shared or sold.

4. Blockchain Privacy Disclaimer

Because blockchain data is public and permanent, you should never post any personal or sensitive data in any hash, comment, or forward message.

5. Your Responsibility

By using Unlockhash.com, you acknowledge and accept the privacy implications of blockchain systems.

6. Contact

Email us at unlockhash@gmail.com with questions or concerns.
